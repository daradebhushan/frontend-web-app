Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Loknagar by TownSeva (the "App"). We provide white-label administrative and grievance redressal software for municipal corporations, local governing bodies, and civic entities. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related services provided on behalf of your local municipal body.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the App, including your name, email address, phone number, and departmental role. When reporting a civic complaint or completing a municipal task, you may provide additional information, such as geographical location and photographic evidence.

3. Device Permissions

To provide core functionalities, the App may request access to:

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used exclusively to capture or upload images for complaint verification, civic issue reporting, and task completion documentation.
  • Location Services: Used to accurately tag the geographical coordinates of reported civic issues (e.g., potholes, streetlight outages) so field workers can locate them.

We do not access or use these permissions for any purpose other than facilitating your interactions within the App and routing data to the appropriate municipal department.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect or receive to:

  • Facilitate account creation and secure logon processes.
  • Manage, prioritize, and route civic complaints to the appropriate government departments (e.g., Water Supply, City Cleanliness, Property Tax).
  • Communicate with you regarding updates on your submitted tasks or complaints.
  • Ensure the security and internal operation of our civic application infrastructure.

5. Sharing Your Information

We act as a data processor for local municipal bodies. Your data is strictly used for internal civic administration, field operations, and grievance routing. We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any of your personal information with third-party advertisers or commercial entities for promotional purposes.

6. Security of Your Information

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational secure cloud infrastructure (including role-based access control and JWT authentication) to protect your personal information. However, please remember that we cannot guarantee the internet itself is 100% secure.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this policy, or wish to exercise your data rights, you may contact the system administrator, your designated municipal department head, or the local civic body utilizing this software platform. You may also contact our support team directly at noreply@townseva.in.
