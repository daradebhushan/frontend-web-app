App Support

Loknagar by TownSeva

1. Need Help?

If you are experiencing any issues with the Loknagar application, require assistance with your account, or have questions about how to use the app for administrative and grievance redressal tasks, our support team is here to help.

2. Contact Information

Email Support: support@townseva.in

Please include the following information in your email to help us resolve your issue faster:

  • Your municipal department and role
  • A detailed description of the issue or question
  • Any relevant screenshots or error messages
  • The type of device you are using (e.g., iPhone 13, iOS 17)

3. Response Time

Our support team actively monitors inquiries during regular business hours. We strive to respond to all support requests within 24-48 hours. For urgent administrative matters, please reach out directly to your designated municipal department head.
