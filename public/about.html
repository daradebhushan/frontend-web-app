About TownSeva (Loknagar)

Transforming Civic Governance & Citizen Services

1. Our Vision

TownSeva (Loknagar) is a next-generation civic technology platform designed specifically for Municipal Councils (Nagar Parishads and Nagar Panchayats) across India. Our mission is to bridge the gap between citizens and local government administration through smart digital communication, transparent grievance redressal, and efficient field operations.

2. What We Provide

💬 Citizen WhatsApp Redressal

Empowering citizens to report civic grievances (Garbage clearance, Drainage, Water supply disruptions, Street light failures, and Road repair) directly via an intelligent, multilingual (Marathi & English) WhatsApp Chatbot without downloading complex applications.

📱 Field Staff & Officer Mobile App

Providing municipal field staff and department heads with an intuitive mobile task management tool to receive instant issue assignments, update progress in real-time, and verify completed tasks with photographic evidence.

📊 Municipal Administration Dashboard

Giving Chief Officers and Municipal Presidents complete operational visibility over municipal KPIs, staff accountability, resolution turnaround times, and citizen satisfaction ratings.

3. Transparency & Security

TownSeva uses enterprise-grade cloud security, strict role-based access control, and end-to-end data encryption to safeguard municipal records and citizen identity.
